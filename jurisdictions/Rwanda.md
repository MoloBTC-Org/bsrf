## Executive Summary

Rwanda enacted **Law No. 023/2026 of 25 May 2026 regulating virtual asset business**, published in Official Gazette n° Special of **28 May 2026** and in force on publication. Official text: [CMA-hosted Gazette PDF](https://www.cma.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/law_regulating_virtual_asset_business.pdf).

The Capital Market Authority is the lead licensing authority. The National Bank of Rwanda retains a role where activity touches payments, monetary stability or systemic risk. Virtual assets may not be used as a means of payment unless the central bank expressly authorises it. Only incorporated legal entities may provide virtual asset services. Implementing regulations were still being written as of mid-2026. Self-custody by individuals is not the licensing target.

## 2. Current Regulatory Overview

### Licensing
CMA licences exchanges, custodians, brokers, issuance, tokenisation and related services. Applicants must be incorporated in Rwanda or registered with a local presence; ownership and fit-and-proper tests apply. Unlicensed operation carries reported penalties of RWF 30–50 million and imprisonment of up to five years.

### Payments
Use of virtual assets as a means of payment requires BNR authorisation. That is a payments-perimeter rule, not a ban on holding.

### Reporting
The law includes user-identification requirements for virtual asset transfers (Travel Rule-style obligations on providers).

### Self-custody
Natural persons may not operate a virtual asset *business* without a licence. Private holding of a key is a different act and is not framed as the licensed activity.

## 5. Philosophical & Legal Synthesis

Rwanda chose a standalone virtual-asset statute rather than stretching securities or payments law. CMA-as-lead implies an investment-product view. BNR’s payment veto is the monetary-sovereignty reservation. Neither agency has enacted a Bitcoin carve-out. The Framework argument still has to be made at the self-custody and node boundary.

## 6. Adoption Implications

- Service businesses need a CMA licence and a Rwandan legal person.
- Individuals holding Bitcoin are outside that licence until a regulation says otherwise.
- Domestic payment in Bitcoin is not a default right.

## Truth & Clarity Summary

Law 023/2026 is in force. It licenses virtual-asset *business*. It does not prohibit self-custody and it does not treat Bitcoin as legal tender. Implementing rules will decide how tight the perimeter becomes.
