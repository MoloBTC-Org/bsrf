## Executive Summary

On **18 July 2026**, the Government of Kazakhstan approved **Resolution No. 638**, establishing the rules for **“strategic digital mining.”** The rules take effect on **1 August 2026**.

Under this regime, large-scale miners that meet high infrastructure thresholds may obtain preferential electricity access (capped tariffs under contracts of up to 10 years). In exchange, they must transfer a portion of their mined digital assets each month — calculated after deducting electricity and transmission costs (including VAT) — to the Astana Hub Autonomous Cluster Fund. Those assets are then placed under the trust management of the National Investment Corporation of the National Bank of Kazakhstan for investment in the **National Strategic Crypto Reserve**.

Local reporting consistently describes the contribution as approximately **10% of the remaining mined assets** after power costs. The National Strategic Crypto Reserve is designed to be capitalised without the use of public funds, relying instead on voluntary participation by strategic miners.

This framework converts Kazakhstan’s position as a major global Bitcoin mining jurisdiction into a dual outcome: continued private-sector hashrate activity plus a direct mechanism for the state to accumulate digital assets.

---

## 1. Context

Kazakhstan ranks among the top five jurisdictions globally by Bitcoin mining activity (Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance, 2025 data). After the 2021 Chinese mining ban, the country absorbed significant hashrate, primarily utilising surplus coal-fired generation.

The strategic mining regime sits alongside, but is distinct from, the broader legislative framework for digital assets (see companion document *Kazakhstan – Legislative Framework*). It is an industrial-policy instrument rather than a general regulatory statute.

---

## 2. Core Mechanism of Resolution No. 638

**Effective date:** 1 August 2026

**Exchange structure:**
- Preferential electricity quotas at tariffs not exceeding the maximum rate set for electricity producers.
- Contract duration: up to 10 years.
- Mandatory monthly transfer of a share of mined digital assets (after power and transmission costs) to dedicated wallets of the Astana Hub Autonomous Cluster Fund.
- Assets subsequently transferred to the National Investment Corporation of the National Bank for management within the National Strategic Crypto Reserve.

**Initial power allocation:**
- Ekibastuz GRES-1 allocated a 300 MW quota for strategic digital mining.

**Eligibility thresholds (high bar):**
- Ownership of a digital mining data centre with capacity of at least **150 MW**.
- Mining hardware where each unit delivers a minimum of **150 TH/s**.
- Qualified technical staff and on-site repair facilities.
- Dual independent telecommunications contracts.
- No outstanding mandatory payments or property encumbrances.
- Technical conditions for connection from substations of 35 kV and above.

Participation is voluntary. Miners may combine strategic digital mining with ordinary digital mining activity.

---

## 3. National Strategic Crypto Reserve

The Reserve is intended to hold and invest in:
- Digital assets
- Derivatives linked to digital assets
- Equity in companies that build or invest in the crypto sector

It is explicitly designed to grow without direct budgetary expenditure, relying on the contribution stream from strategic miners.

---

## 4. Implications for Bitcoin

- **Industrial concentration**: The high eligibility thresholds favour large operators. Smaller miners remain outside the preferential power regime.
- **State accumulation**: The model creates a direct pipeline from private hashrate into a sovereign digital asset holding, without requiring the state to purchase Bitcoin on the open market.
- **Self-custody**: The regime does not address or restrict individual self-custody. Its scope is limited to industrial mining participants that elect to join the strategic programme.
- **Energy policy linkage**: Preferential long-term power contracts are the primary commercial incentive. The parallel presidential decree also enables the use of associated petroleum gas and natural gas for autonomous generation dedicated to mining where such gas is not required for state needs.

---

## 5. Relationship to the Broader Legislative Framework

This specialised regime operates under the umbrella of the Law on Digital Assets and the July 2026 presidential decree, but it is not a general licensing or tax statute. It is a targeted industrial-policy tool aimed at large-scale mining and sovereign asset accumulation.

For the full legislative and tax treatment of digital assets (including the planned personal income tax exemption and licensing of exchanges), refer to the companion document *Kazakhstan – Legislative Framework*.

---

## 6. Monitoring Points

- Precise contribution formula and any official confirmation of the reported 10% rate.
- Expansion of electricity quotas beyond the initial Ekibastuz GRES-1 allocation.
- Practical operation and investment mandate of the National Strategic Crypto Reserve.
- Number and identity of miners granted strategic status.
- Interaction with ordinary digital mining licensing and energy quota rules.

---

**Truth & Clarity Summary**  
Kazakhstan’s Strategic Digital Mining regime (Resolution No. 638, effective 1 August 2026) exchanges long-term preferential electricity for a share of mined digital assets directed into a National Strategic Crypto Reserve. It is a sovereign accumulation strategy layered on top of the country’s position as a major mining jurisdiction. It does not alter the general legal treatment of self-custody or retail trading.
