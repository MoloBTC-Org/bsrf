## Executive Summary

Zimbabwe brought virtual assets into the financial-crime and financial-services perimeter in two steps. The **Finance Act, 2025** recognised virtual assets and VASPs. **Statutory Instrument 99 of 2026** — the Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime (Virtual Asset Service Providers Registration) Regulations, 2026 — was gazetted on **10 June 2026**. Official text: [Veritas copy of S.I. 99](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202026-099%20Money%20Laundering%20and%20Proceeds%20of%20Crime%20%28Virtual%20Asset%20Service%20Providers%20Registration%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf).

Registration is with the Financial Intelligence Unit. The FIU has stated in terms that **registration is not a licence to operate**. RBZ and SECZim approvals remain separate. Applicants must be established as a Zimbabwean legal person. Registration issuance fee is US$500. Self-custody by individuals is not the registered activity.

## 1. Historical Thread

- December 2025 Finance Act: virtual assets and VASPs recognised; financial-institution definition widened.
- **10 June 2026**: S.I. 99 of 2026 gazetted.
- Mid-June 2026 FIU public notice: registration ≠ operating authority; enquiries to vasps@fiu.co.zw.
- Parallel track: Victoria Falls International Financial Services Centre rules for tokenised and blockchain activity.

## 2. Current Regulatory Overview

### Registration
No person may operate as a VASP in or from Zimbabwe unless registered with the FIU on the prescribed form. Covered activities follow the FATF list: exchange against fiat, exchange between virtual assets, transfer, custody/administration, and financial services around issuance or sale.

### Other regulators
SECZim and the Reserve Bank remain relevant for securities-like products and banking access. VFIFSC is a specialised centre, not the mainland default.

### Self-custody
Holding a key is not FIU registration. Providing custody or exchange *as a business* is.

## 5. Philosophical & Legal Synthesis

S.I. 99 is an AML registration statute. It is not a monetary-recognition statute and it is not a securities classification of Bitcoin. The FIU’s own distinction — registration versus licence — should be preserved in any Framework reading. Self-custody remains the unregistered act.

## 6. Adoption Implications

- Service businesses need a local company and an FIU registration, then whatever RBZ/SECZim add.
- Individuals can still hold keys; they cannot lawfully run an unregistered exchange.

## Truth & Clarity Summary

Zimbabwe now registers VASPs for AML purposes under S.I. 99 of 2026. That is not a finding that Bitcoin is a security, legal tender, or *sui generis* property. Self-custody is not the registered activity.
