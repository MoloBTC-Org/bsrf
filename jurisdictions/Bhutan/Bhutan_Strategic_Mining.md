## Executive Summary

Bhutan is one of the earliest and most distinctive examples of a sovereign state deliberately converting surplus renewable energy into Bitcoin as a long-term strategic asset.

Through Druk Holding & Investments (DHI), the government has conducted hydro-powered Bitcoin mining since approximately 2019. The resulting holdings have, at various points, ranked Bhutan among the larger known nation-state Bitcoin holders relative to the size of its economy.

In December 2025, His Majesty the King announced the allocation of up to **10,000 BTC** from national holdings as a long-term strategic reserve to support the development of Gelephu Mindfulness City (GMC). In July 2026, GMC appointed the Canadian digital-asset manager 3iQ to manage a portion of that Bitcoin treasury.

Mining is powered exclusively by hydropower. Official policy prioritises domestic consumers and other industries over mining for electricity allocation, particularly during the dry season.

---

## 1. State-Backed Mining Model

- Mining operations are conducted under the sovereign investment arm **Druk Holding & Investments (DHI)**.
- Energy source: surplus hydropower (glacier-fed rivers). Bhutan has emphasised that the activity is consistent with its carbon-negative and environmental commitments.
- Facilities have included sites at Gedu (approx. 100 MW) and a larger facility at Jigmeling (reported up to 500 MW capacity at full operation).
- Mining is flexible: operations can be scaled down when hydropower generation is seasonally constrained so that priority users retain access to electricity.

This model treats excess renewable energy as an input that can be stored in the form of Bitcoin rather than left unutilised or sold at low marginal value.

---

## 2. Sovereign Bitcoin Holdings and the GMC Allocation

- Bhutan accumulated a substantial Bitcoin position through mining. Peak estimates from on-chain analytics placed holdings in the region of 13,000 BTC in late 2024.
- Subsequent on-chain activity in 2025–2026 indicated significant outflows and sales, reducing reported holdings. Exact current figures are not officially disclosed and estimates vary.
- **December 2025 National Day Address**: His Majesty announced that up to 10,000 BTC from the Bitcoin mining project would be allocated to Gelephu Mindfulness City as a long-term strategic reserve to secure the project’s financial foundation and preserve benefits for the nation.
- **July 2026**: GMC appointed 3iQ to manage an undisclosed portion of the Bitcoin allocated to the city. The appointment includes commitments to local presence, talent development, and knowledge transfer.

The stated intention is long-term holding and responsible management (including potential use as collateral under strict governance rules) rather than short-term liquidation of the entire reserve.

---

## 3. Relationship to the Broader Regulatory Framework

The mining and sovereign holdings activity is distinct from the retail and commercial regulatory regime (see companion document *Bhutan – Legislative Framework*).

- State mining and holdings are conducted through sovereign entities (DHI and GMC-related structures).
- Private commercial mining and exchange activity is restricted to entities operating under the GMC framework.
- The 10,000 BTC allocation to GMC represents the deployment of sovereign mining proceeds into a national development project, managed with institutional partners.

---

## 4. Implications for Bitcoin

- **Sovereign accumulation**: Bhutan demonstrated an early, deliberate strategy of converting renewable energy surplus into a non-sovereign monetary asset held on the national balance sheet.
- **Clean energy narrative**: The exclusive use of hydropower distinguishes Bhutan’s model from fossil-fuel-dependent mining jurisdictions.
- **Treasury management evolution**: The move from pure accumulation to the appointment of an external institutional manager (3iQ) for a portion of the GMC allocation signals a shift toward professionalised, long-term reserve management.
- **Self-custody**: The specialised document does not alter the legal position of individual self-custody, which remains governed by the dual RMA/GMC framework described in the legislative companion document.

---

## 5. Monitoring Points

- Official disclosure (if any) of current sovereign Bitcoin holdings and the precise amount placed under 3iQ management.
- Continuity and scale of state mining operations (on-chain inflow data has indicated possible slowdowns).
- Governance and investment mandate applied to the GMC strategic reserve.
- Any further sovereign allocations or policy statements linking mining revenues to national development priorities (including reported historical linkages to civil servant compensation).
- Interaction between energy priority rules and mining capacity during dry-season constraints.

---

**Truth & Clarity Summary**  
Bhutan’s model is one of the clearest examples of a state treating Bitcoin mining as a strategic conversion of surplus renewable energy into a long-term national asset. The December 2025 allocation of up to 10,000 BTC to Gelephu Mindfulness City, followed by the July 2026 appointment of 3iQ as manager of a portion of that treasury, marks the transition from pure accumulation to institutionalised sovereign reserve management. This specialised activity sits alongside, but is distinct from, the dual-track legislative regime that confines private commercial crypto activity primarily to GMC.
