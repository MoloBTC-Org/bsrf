## Executive Summary

Zambia does **not** yet have a comprehensive VASP statute. On **16 March 2026** the Bank of Zambia issued a public notice requiring all persons providing virtual-asset services in or into Zambia to **register by 27 March 2026**. Official notice: [BoZ PDF](https://www.boz.zm/Public_Notice_Registration_of_VASPs_Operating_in_Zambia.pdf). From **30 March 2026**, BoZ-regulated institutions may not facilitate transactions with unregistered VASPs. Registration is expressly **not a licence**. A full framework was described as forthcoming.

Self-custody by individuals is not the registered activity.

## 1. Historical Thread

- No dedicated virtual-asset Act in force as of September 2026.
- **16 March 2026**: BoZ public notice; Microsoft Forms registration link.
- **25 March 2026**: CB Circular No. 5 of 2026 on mandatory registration of virtual-asset and stablecoin service providers.
- **30 March 2026**: banking-channel cutoff for unregistered VASPs.

## 2. Current Regulatory Overview

### Registration only
Covered activities copy the FATF VASP list and apply to residents and non-residents serving the Zambian public, with or without a local office.

### Future statute
BoZ has said a comprehensive regulatory framework will follow and that registered VASPs will have to comply with it to continue operating.

### Self-custody
Holding a key is not “providing virtual/crypto asset services … as a business.”

## 3. Key Players

- Bank of Zambia
- Other regulators named in the notice only as “collaborating stakeholders”

## 4. Platforms for Advocacy

- BoZ consultation on the promised comprehensive framework.
- Banking-channel practice after 30 March 2026.

## 5. Philosophical & Legal Synthesis

This is a census and a payments-rail lock, not a classification of Bitcoin. Until a statute exists, Zambia should not be described as a licensed VASP jurisdiction.

## 6. Adoption Implications

- Service providers that want bank rails must register.
- Individuals holding Bitcoin are outside the notice.

## Next Steps

1. Capture the comprehensive framework the day it is published.
2. Do not upgrade this file to “licensed regime” on the basis of the March notice alone.

## Truth & Clarity Summary

Zambia has a 2026 registration notice and a banking cutoff. It does not yet have a VASP Act. Self-custody is untouched by the notice.
